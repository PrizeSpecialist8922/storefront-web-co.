FAQ

Common questions

Do you charge for quotes?

New-system quotes are free. Repairs start with a diagnostic fee that is credited if you go ahead with the repair.

How fast can you get here?

Most repair calls are booked same or next day. Emergencies are prioritized any time, day or night.

Are there rebates for heat pumps?

Programs change often, so we check what's available for your home and handle the paperwork with you.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Parts and labour are covered on every job, and installs carry the manufacturer's warranty too.
